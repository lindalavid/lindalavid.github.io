A note about this collection

We recently compiled some digital abstract pages into a book, Papers for Abstract Art: Geometrics, Color Tones and Lines. Here’s our book blurb…”Forty unique, premium-color, abstract designs for individual or group mixed media projects. Designs are carefully selected for work that highlights geometrics, color tones, and lines that will serve in creating and augmenting any representational or non-representational artwork. Perfect for beginner and seasoned artists of every age who are looking to ignite their creativity and play with form, color, and composition.”
