Bistro

Pull up a chair. Expressive color, intimate interiors, and the pleasure of seeing ordinary things anew.

A note about this collection

In this series, we embraced the raw vitality and liberated approach of expressionism, finding joy in the exploration of art's more visceral and instinctual capacities. Delighting in the creative process, we allowed our artistic impulses to lead the way, resulting in works that are unrestrained, exuberant, and teeter on the edge of the tangible world. Each piece is an ode to the dance of light and shadow, an experiment in how hues interact and textures emerge to evoke emotion and tell a story without the confines of strict form.
Oh, my such fun!!!
This series holds a special place in our hearts since it was when we became artists.
Stay loose, my friends!
