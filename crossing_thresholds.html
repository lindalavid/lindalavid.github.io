Images Found in Crossing Thresholds

Our Journey into AI Creativity

In the same way that painting once opened a new path for us, so too has the world of artificial intelligence. These images were generated using AI—not as a replacement for imagination, but as an extension of it. Each one feels like a doorway into something unexpected, reminding us that creativity can come from many places. We offer them here for personal use—whether you’d like to add them to your journal, keep them as digital wallpapers, or adapt them for your own art projects. They are free to use and share, meant simply to inspire reflection, play, and wonder.    Please enjoy and use freely for non-commercial purposes.
