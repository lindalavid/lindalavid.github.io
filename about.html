The person behind the pages & paintings

Hello, I’m Linda.

Author, mixed-media artist, and the imagination behind Mrs. Plum’s Art Emporium.

And who, exactly, is Mrs. Plum?

By day, we navigate the ordinary world, finding joy in organizing and a sparkle in a bit of glitter. By night, Mrs. Plum takes over: a character who might have stepped out of a board game, with a flair for drama and an oversized magnifying glass for “research.”

Our headquarters is the plum barn, a place for paintbrushes, stories, experiments, and the occasional conversation with a furred companion. It’s where one idea often turns into something entirely different.

Creativity turns every day into a puzzle waiting to be solved.

A place to explore.

The collections here bring together painting, collage, mixed media, and explorations with AI. Each is another way of following an idea and seeing where it leads.

I share digital images for personal, non-commercial use, whether you’re looking for inspiration, adding to a journal, or making something of your own. Make yourself at home.
