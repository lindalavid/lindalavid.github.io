A note about this collection

With over fifteen years of experience in the art world, we have recently embraced the practice of art journaling, a decision that, in retrospect, seems long overdue. Our latest mixed media work, featured below, exemplifies the profound benefits of journaling as a method for artists to explore and refine their unique artistic voice. The simplicity of art journaling cannot be overstated – all one requires is a journal and a pencil. The subject matter can range from basic shapes to more elaborate drawings, tracings, or any form of markings. The images presented below are intended to serve as inspiration or a starting point for your own journaling journey. The core essence of art journaling lies in the joy of exploration and the freedom of expression it offers. It is an invitation to discover and develop your artistic sensibility in a fun and engaging manner.
